How it looks on the bench

Power enters the box through an IEC C8 (figure‑8) inlet — no earth. Only two WAGOs: one for all browns, one for all blues.

Parts for mains: C8 inlet → 2× WAGO 221-415 → (1) cut projector cord, (2) female EU socket for official Pi PSU, (3) Mean Well AC in.
No 221-413 / no yellow-green. C8 has nowhere to bring earth in.
EU colours: brown = live · blue = neutral. Strip ~11 mm. Power off while splicing. Class II only on this feed (Pi brick, Mean Well, projector).

Overview

Step 1 — Live (brown): one 221-415

Step 2 — Neutral (blue): the other 221-415

Step 3 — LED 5V (sockerbitar, not WAGO)

Mounting (lid / ceiling)

Mean Well: screw through its holes.
WAGOs: hot-glue the plastic body to the wood (not the levers); zip-tie cables for strain relief.
Pi PSU: velcro or zip-tie through wood — don’t hang it only on sticky cable clips.
Female EU: fix so plugging the brick in doesn’t yank the WAGO wires.
Done when: C8 → 2 orange 415s (brown bundle + blue bundle) → projector / female‑EU / Mean Well; Mean Well 5V → sockerbitar → LED red+black; Pi GND bonded; LED yellow → pin 12; official Pi PSU plugged into the female. No earth WAGO.
